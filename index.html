How signals work
  • Timing: no signal is created before 9:30 AM IST. New signals are created until 2:30 PM. It is intraday: assume you exit by 3:15 PM.
  • Locked: a signal's Entry, Stop and Targets are saved with the time and never change on refresh. Each stock gets at most one signal per day.
  • Direction check: a call (CE) needs the stock above its day open and up on the day, with Daily and 15m ratings both bullish. A put (PE) is the mirror image.
  • Entry: take the trade only after the stock trades through the Entry level. If it never does, the result is "No entry".
  • Stop is 0.4 ATR beyond entry. T1 / T2 are 0.6 and 1.0 ATR in your favour. The scorecard assumes you book everything at T1.
  • Live tab: shows entered trades, top 5 by profit %, with hold / book / exit confirmations.
  • Grade adds one point each for: strong combined rating, volume 1.5x or more, 5m agreeing, move not stretched, price near the trigger. A = 4+, B = 3, C = fewer.
How the hold / book / exit confirmation works
  • Each running trade is checked against live indicators: 5m and 15m TradingView ratings, price versus VWAP, MACD (5m), RSI (5m), and how much of the peak profit has been given back.
  • Each indicator supports your direction (+), opposes it (-) or is neutral. The count of supporting indicators is the confirmation score. It is a checklist, not a probability.
  • HOLD: profit or plan intact and most confirmations support it. BOOK PROFIT: profit exists but momentum is fading, price gave back a lot, RSI is stretched, or Target 1 is reached without strong support. EXIT NOW: price is at the stop, or confirmations have turned against a trade that is not in profit.
  • Suggested trailing stop: original stop first. After half the distance to Target 1, move it to entry. After Target 1, it locks about 70% of the Target 1 profit.
  • If VWAP, MACD or RSI are missing, the check uses fewer indicators. These are rules of thumb, not guarantees, and indicators lag fast reversals.
Accuracy so far

Direction accuracy = signals where the close ended on the signalled side of the signal price (counts every locked signal, even if entry never triggered). Win rate = triggered trades that hit Target 1, or closed in profit. 1R = the risk to the stop. Prices are sampled while this page is open, plus the day's high, low and close fetched after the close. When Stop and Target 1 both fall in the day's range and the order is unknown, it is scored as a loss. Paper results ignore slippage and brokerage.
