How signals work
  • Timing: no signal is created before 9:30 AM IST (the first 15 minutes are noise). New signals are created until 2:30 PM. It is intraday: assume you exit by 3:15 PM.
  • Locked: when a signal is created, its Entry, Stop and Targets are saved on this device with the time. They never change on refresh. Each stock gets at most one signal per day.
  • Direction check: a call (CE) needs the stock above its day open and up on the day, with Daily and 15m ratings both bullish. A put (PE) is the mirror image.
  • Entry: take the trade only after the stock trades through the Entry level (the day's high for CE, low for PE at signal time). If it never does, the result is "No entry".
  • Stop is 0.4 ATR beyond entry. T1 / T2 are 0.6 and 1.0 ATR in your favour. The scorecard assumes you book everything at T1.
  • Results: after 3:30 PM the Track tab shows, for each signal, the move from signal price to close, whether Entry / Target / Stop was hit, and the accuracy.
  • The % under each level is the stock move. The "option" % multiplies it by the leverage you set. Real option P/L depends on strike, expiry, IV and spread.
  • Grade adds one point each for: strong combined rating, volume 1.5x or more, 5m agreeing, move not stretched, price near the trigger. A = 4+, B = 3, C = fewer.
Accuracy so far

Direction accuracy = signals where the close ended on the signalled side of the signal price (counts every locked signal, even if entry never triggered). Win rate = triggered trades that hit Target 1, or closed in profit. 1R = the risk to the stop. Prices are sampled while this page is open, plus the day's high, low and close fetched after the close. When Stop and Target 1 both fall in the day's range and the order is unknown, it is scored as a loss. Paper results ignore slippage and brokerage.
