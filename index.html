How signals work
  • Timing: no signal is created before 9:30 AM IST or after 2:30 PM. Between 11:30 AM and 1:30 PM (lunch chop) only Grade A signals are created. Every trade is squared off at 3:15 PM.
  • Locked: a signal's Entry, Stop and Targets are saved with the time and never change on refresh. Each stock gets at most one signal per day.
  • Early-momentum check: scans all NSE F&O stocks for fresh momentum. A call (CE) needs both 5m and 15m ratings clearly bullish (≥ 0.2, avg ≥ 0.3), Daily not bearish, mandatory institutional volume (≥ 1.3x normal), price above open, up on the day, above VWAP, and tight near the breakout high (≤ 0.6%). A put (PE) is the mirror image.
  • Fuel & stretch caps: stocks that have already moved more than 1.0x daily ATR or sit more than 0.5 ATR away from VWAP are skipped so you do not enter after momentum is exhausted.
  • Top 5 & Market filter: strictly capped at the Top 5 highest-ranked stocks per day in any CE/PE mix (e.g. 1 CE + 4 PE or 3 CE + 2 PE). Counter-trend picks against NIFTY bias must be exceptional (Grade A + ≥ 1.8x volume), or you can block them completely in settings. India VIX 20+ = A grade only.
  • Relative strength & sector cap: a CE stock must beat both NIFTY and its sector average today (mirror for PE). With Sector Cap on (default), at most 1 active trade per sector is allowed at a time.
  • Daily limits: strictly capped at 5 signals per day, max 3 open/waiting at once, and automatic stop after 2 stop-losses or -2R closed. Grade A only by default; Grade C is never signalled.
  • Entry: starts only when the stock trades through Entry and is on the right side of VWAP. If price is already > 0.2 ATR past Entry when first seen, it is marked "Missed, did not chase". Expires after 90 minutes.
  • Stop: 0.7 ATR beyond entry so normal noise does not shake you out. When VWAP sits 0.4 to 0.9 ATR behind entry, the stop goes just beyond VWAP instead. Signals risking over 3% on the stock are skipped.
  • Targets and exits: T1 = 1.5R, T2 = 2.5R. Original stop stays intact until T1 is hit; at T1, half is booked and the stop on the rest moves to breakeven. Time stop after 60 minutes if T1 is not reached. Scorecard deducts your slippage/cost setting (default 0.20R) from closed trades.
  • Index signals (NIFTY / SENSEX only): tracked separately from the Top 5 F&O stocks (max 1 index trade/day to avoid doubling correlated risk). Requires ADX(14) 5m ≥ 20, EMA 10/20 5m trend stack, RSI 5m in momentum zone (55–72 CE / 28–45 PE), F&O breadth (≥ 60% CE / ≤ 40% PE), Bank Nifty not opposing, and India VIX ≥ 11.5. Uses 0.4 ATR stop, 1.0R / 1.8R targets, 30-min time-stop, 1 OTM strike (50-pt NIFTY, 100-pt SENSEX), and blocks new afternoon entries after 1:30 PM on expiry day (Tue NIFTY, Thu SENSEX).
  • Strike and size: stocks suggest 1 strike ITM (less decay than ATM); NIFTY & SENSEX suggest 1 strike OTM. Quantity = (capital x risk %) / (delta x stop distance). Divide by the lot size from your broker to get lots. Always verify the option bid-ask spread in your broker app before entering.
  • Grade adds one point each for: strong 5m+15m momentum (≥ 0.5), high volume (≥ 1.8x), Daily agreeing, fresh move (≤ 0.75 ATR), and tight at trigger (≤ 0.25%). A = 4+, B = 3, C = fewer.
How the hold / book / exit confirmation works
  • Each running trade is checked against live indicators: 5m and 15m TradingView ratings, price versus VWAP, MACD (5m), RSI (5m), and how much of the peak profit has been given back.
  • Each indicator supports your direction (+), opposes it (-) or is neutral. The count of supporting indicators is the confirmation score. It is a checklist, not a probability.
  • HOLD: profit or plan intact and most confirmations support it. BOOK PROFIT: profit exists but momentum is fading, price gave back a lot, RSI is stretched, or Target 1 is reached without strong support. EXIT NOW: price is at the stop, or confirmations have turned against a trade that is not in profit.
  • Suggested trailing stop: original stop stays intact until Target 1 (1.5R) is actually reached (no premature breakeven move that chokes winners). After Target 1, half is booked and the stop on the rest moves to entry (breakeven).
  • If VWAP, MACD or RSI are missing, the check uses fewer indicators. These are rules of thumb, not guarantees, and indicators lag fast reversals.
Accuracy so far

Direction accuracy = signals where the close ended on the signalled side of the signal price (counts every locked signal, even if entry never triggered). Win rate = triggered trades that closed in net profit after the slippage & costs haircut (default 0.20R, editable in Signals → Risk settings). 1R = the risk to the stop. Prices are sampled while this page is open, plus the day's high, low and close fetched after the close. When Stop and Target 1 both fall in the day's range and the order is unknown, it is scored as a loss. Keep the page open, or open it after 3:30 PM, so results are filled in.
