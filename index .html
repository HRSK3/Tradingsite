NSE Option Scanner

Find breakout and trending stocks, then check them before you trade.

Breakout check
  1. Pick stocks from Gainers or Volume with a big move.
  2. Tap a stock name, then open Analyze.
  3. Want 5m and 15m both on Buy or Strong buy.
  4. Open Chart and look for a clean break of a recent high.

Buy CE = buy call (stock rallying). Buy PE = buy put (stock falling). Levels are on the stock price, built from TradingView ratings and daily ATR, not option premiums. Confirm strike and premium in your broker. Not advice.

The dial combines moving averages and oscillators. Strong buy on several timeframes together suggests a trend with momentum.
